How to convert ChatGPT to PDF

  1. 1

    Open the ChatGPT conversation you want to export

    Any conversation that has been shared via ChatGPT's built-in share feature will work.

  2. 2

    Share the conversation and copy the link

    In ChatGPT, click Share → Copy link. You'll get a URL like https://chatgpt.com/share/…

  3. 3

    Paste the link above and click Convert

    The server fetches the conversation and generates a properly formatted PDF.

  4. 4

    Download your PDF

    Once conversion is complete, a download button appears. Your PDF is ready immediately — no email required.

What formatting is preserved?

The PDF renderer processes the conversation's structured content and reproduces the following elements faithfully:

  • Headings
  • Paragraphs
  • Bullet lists
  • Code blocks
  • Blockquotes
  • Bold / italic
  • Images
  • Attachments
  • Page numbers
  • Source URL
